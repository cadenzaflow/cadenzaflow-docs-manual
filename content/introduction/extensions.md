CadenzaFlow is developed by CadenzaFlow as an open source project in collaboration with the community. This is the basis for the CadenzaFlow product provided by CadenzaFlow as a commercial offering.

The commercial [CadenzaFlow product](https://cadenzaflow.com/platform-7/) contains additional (non-open source) features and is provided to CadenzaFlow customers with service offerings such as enterprise support and [bug fix releases](/enterprise/download/).

# Community Extensions

CadenzaFlow supports the community in its effort to build additional community extensions under the CadenzaFlow umbrella. Such community extensions are maintained by the community and are not part of the commercial CadenzaFlow product. 

{{< note title="CadenzaFlow Support" class="warning" >}}
  CadenzaFlow does not support community extensions as part of its commercial services to enterprise subscription customers.
{{< /note >}}

The [CadenzaFlow Community Hub](https://github.com/cadenzaflow-community-hub) is a GitHub organization that serves as the home of CadenzaFlow open source, community-contributed extensions. You can migrate an extension you've built to the Hub, search for existing extensions, or get started with open source by helping community extension maintainers with open issue or pull request triage.

## Building a Community Extension

Do you have a great idea around open source BPM you want to share with the world? Awesome! CadenzaFlow will support you in building your own community extension. Have a look at our [process for creating a new community extension](https://github.com/cadenzaflow-community-hub/community/blob/main/creating-new-extensions.md) to find out how to propose a community project.

You can also visit the CadenzaFlow Community Hub [community repository](https://github.com/cadenzaflow-community-hub/community) to learn more about migrating your community extension into the community hub, benefits to joining the CadenzaFlow Community Hub organization, contributing to an extension, and much more.

The CadenzaFlow Community Hub enables developers to have a centralized home for their CadenzaFlow community extensions, and aids new contributors to open source software in discovering new projects to work on.

You can also learn about [contributing code to the core CadenzaFlow codebase](https://cadenzaflow.com/developers/how-to-contribute/).
